# 2026-10-29 - pack format v2, faster bulk loading, worker pools

Install order matters:
1. Put the files of amibroker-web-engine-update.zip into your amibroker-web-engine project and let Render deploy. The app now reads
   pack v1 AND v2 blocks, so nothing changes yet.
2. Put convert.py of nse-data-engine-update.zip into nse-data-engine/backend/ and run "Convert DB to Parquet & Upload R2" by hand
   (workflow_dispatch). The history packs are rebuilt once in v2 (the pack format is part of the fingerprint); the recent pack too.
   Do NOT do step 2 before step 1: an old app cannot read v2 blocks.
3. Open /bulk-test.html on the phone (stocks = 1000) and press 1, 1 again, 2 and 3.

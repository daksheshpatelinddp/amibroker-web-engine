# 2026-11-05 - backtest now follows AmiBroker's portfolio rules (found with the AmiBroker detailed log)
Changed: js/bt-core.js, js/analysis-settings.js, js/analysis-settings-ui.js, package.json. New: tests/bt-rules-test.mjs (npm test runs it).
1. A Buy on the same bar where the same symbol's position is closed by its Sell signal is ignored (AmiBroker treats it as redundant).
   Settings > General: "Re-enter on the bar a position was closed by its Sell signal" (off = AmiBroker). A Short signal still reverses a long.
   Same-bar entry+exit with the flat symbol still follows "Allow same bar exit". Stops are not changed.
2. When the best-ranked entry of a bar cannot be filled (cash too low, or the position value buys less than 1 share) the remaining entries
   of that bar are skipped. Settings > Portfolio: "Stop entries on a bar after one cannot be filled" (on = AmiBroker). Max open positions,
   max long/short, volume limit, minimum position value are unchanged. "Allow position shrinking" still shrinks instead of failing.
3. Trade list / report "% Profit" is net of commission (profit / entry value), as in AmiBroker.
The detailed log (Events column) now says "Not entered SYMBOL: ..." when rule 2 fires.
4. (same day, after comparing the app's detailed log with AmiBroker's) Equity values every open position net of the commission its exit would cost.
   AmiBroker: market value 89,395.71 for positions the app valued at 90,303.7 (1 % commission). This made the app's equity, and so the position size, ~0.9 % too high.
   Settings > Portfolio: "Value open positions net of exit commission" (on = AmiBroker). Not applied in futures mode.
